# In the game
Open Options and press Delete my data, then confirm. This deletes your player account, your leaderboard name, your contact details, your notification token, your scores on every board, your ticket balance, your upgrades and your purchase record from our online service, and everything the game saved on your device. If you are offline, the device data goes at once and the rest goes the next time the game is online with the same account. The screen tells you which has happened.

# Without the game
If you have uninstalled the game or lost the phone, write to info@counterspell.dev from any address with the subject Delete my Goofy Getaway data, and tell us your leaderboard name. We will delete the account under that name and everything listed above, and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

# What is kept
Tickets you bought are deleted with the rest and cannot be restored. A phone's record that its free show entries were used stays until the end of that month, without your account attached, and a bar from prizes on a device stays for its 12 months. The app store keeps its own record of your purchases under its own policy, and Remove Ads can be restored from the store with Restore purchases. Your Google Play Games or Apple Game Center account is not ours and is left untouched.

The full Privacy Policy is at https://apps.counterspell.dev/goofy-getaway/privacy-policy.
