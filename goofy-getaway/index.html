# About this policy
Goofy Getaway is made and operated by Counterspell Games LLC, which is the data controller for everything described here. Morgan Jay lends the game his name and likeness; he does not receive or handle your data, except where a prize is handed over at a show, as described below. This policy explains what the game sends, who receives it, why, how long it is kept, and how you can have it deleted. It covers the game only, not the app store you got it from and not any site you reach from it.

The same text is published at https://apps.counterspell.dev/goofy-getaway/privacy-policy. The copy inside the game and the published copy are generated from one file and are identical.

# What we collect
Your runs, your settings and the parts of your progress listed below are saved on your device. These are the things that leave it:

- A player account, created the first time the game reaches our online services, so your scores, tickets and unlocks can be recognised as yours
- Your leaderboard name, the name you choose, checked by our online service when you set it
- Runs: the score and the details of each run, so it can be checked and ranked
- Show nights: the venue code you enter at a show, and the runs you choose to enter on that show's board
- A device check when you check in at a show, so free entries can be limited to one account per phone each month
- Your ticket balance and the upgrades you own, held by our online service so they are not lost with your phone
- Purchases: the store's reference for a purchase, so it can be confirmed and credited once
- Advertising: an advertising identifier held by your device, read by the ad networks that serve ads in the game
- Contact details, only if you add them on the Socials page in Options: an email address or the handles of your social media accounts, so we can reach you if you win a prize, and about shows unless you untick the box for it
- A notification token, only if you allow notifications: an address for the game on this phone, so we can tell you when you win a show board

# What we do not collect
- Your real name, phone number or contacts. We only hold an email address if you add one on the Socials page or write to us
- Analytics or crash reports. The game has no analytics or crash reporting service
- Your location. On a show night the game can compare your phone's approximate location with the venue's, on your phone, to offer the check-in button (see Show nights). Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else. The ad networks can estimate a rough location from your internet address, as described under Advertising
- Payment details. Purchases are handled entirely by the app store, which never gives us a card number
- Your photos, microphone or camera. The game reads none of them

# Your player account
The first time the game reaches our online services, Unity Gaming Services creates an anonymous player id for this installation. It is not your name, your email or a device serial number, and we cannot use it to find out who you are. If you link Google Play Games on Android or Apple Game Center on iOS, the account follows that sign in instead, so your scores, tickets and upgrades come with you to a new phone. The platform sign in belongs to Google or Apple; we receive an id that confirms the link, never your platform password or contacts.

Without a linked platform account, the anonymous id is tied to this installation. Uninstalling, clearing the app's data or moving to a new phone usually means a new id, and anything held under the old one cannot be matched back to you.

# Your leaderboard name
When you choose a name, the game sends it to a small program we run on Unity Gaming Services. It checks the name against a list of words and against names that imitate Morgan Jay or the game, records it so no one else can take it, and attaches it to your scores. A name can be changed once every 30 days. If a name breaks the terms we may reset it to a plain one and ask you to choose again.

# Runs and the leaderboards
When a run ends, the game sends it to our online service, which checks that the score is possible and then ranks it. A run holds your score and the details needed to check it: how long it lasted, how far you ran, the tickets, letters and power ups you picked up, each spin of the prize wheel and what it paid, the lives you had, used and recovered, any revive and how you paid for it, the upgrades you had at the start, how often the game was paused or left the screen, and your device model. A run is filed under your player id with the name you chose.

Your best score, with your name, is shown on the global board to every player. A run that fails our checks is not shown and is kept on a hidden board for review. If the game is offline when a run ends, the run is kept on your device and sent when the game is next online.

# Show nights
At one of Morgan Jay's shows you can check in by typing the code printed at the venue. The code is sent to our online service, which records that your player id is checked in to that show's board for that night. You then choose which runs to enter on the show board: one free entry, one entry for watching a rewarded ad, and further entries for tickets. An entered run is shown on that night's board with your name.

While a show board is open and your phone is in that show's time zone, the game may ask your permission to use your phone's approximate location. If you allow it, the game compares your location with the venue's on your phone, and if you are there it shows a Check in to the show button, which opens the code check-in. Your location is not stored and is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it does not check you in by itself: the code does. If you say no, nothing else changes, and you can still check in with the code from the leaderboard. You can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

To keep free entries fair, checking in also runs a device check:

- On iOS the game asks Apple's DeviceCheck service for a one time token. Apple lets us store two bits of information about the device, which say whether its free entries have been used this month and whether it is barred from prizes. Apple gives us no identifier for the device, and the bits do not identify you.
- On Android the game asks Google Play Integrity to confirm it is the genuine app on a genuine device, and sends your device's Android ID with the request. Our service stores only a keyed hash of that ID, never the ID itself, with the month its free entries were used and any bar from prizes. The Android ID is never given to the ad networks and is never stored beside the advertising identifier.

A device that breaks the official rules can be barred from prizes for 12 months. If you think a bar is wrong, write to us at the address below.

If you win a show board, we tell you in up to three ways: a YOU WON button on the main menu, which stays until the end of the next day; a notification, if you allowed them (see Notifications); and an email, if you added an email address on the Socials page. To hand over the prize, Morgan Jay's tour crew check the YOU WON screen in the game and your ticket for that show at the venue, and in Canada you may be asked to answer a skill testing question. Nothing about you is recorded when the prize is handed over. The official rules for show nights are in the game beside this policy.

# Notifications
Right after your first check in at a show, the game asks whether it may send you notifications. If you allow it, Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging gives the game on this phone a notification token, and the game sends that token to our online service, which keeps it with your player account. We use it only to tell you that you have won a show board. On an iPhone the notification is delivered through Apple's push notification service. If you say no, nothing else changes, and a prize still shows in the game. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

# Tickets, upgrades and the daily reward
Your ticket balance, the upgrades you own and when you last collected the daily reward are kept by our online service under your player id, so a lost phone does not mean lost tickets when your account is linked. Tickets earned in a run are credited when the run is accepted.

# Purchases
Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. When you buy something, the game sends the store's reference for that purchase to our online service, which asks Apple or Google to confirm it and then credits it to your player account once. We receive the product and an order reference, never a card number, name or address. The record of what you bought is kept while your player account exists. The store keeps its own record under its own policy.

# Advertising
Goofy Getaway shows ads, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may fill a request from another ad network it works with on our behalf: AppLovin, DT Exchange, InMobi, ironSource, Liftoff, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Pangle or Unity Ads. When it does, that network receives the ad request, including the advertising identifier your device holds, and serves the ad. Ads appear as a banner on the main menu and sometimes full screen after a run ends. Rewarded ads are optional and you choose when to watch one. Remove Ads takes away the banner and the full screen ads; the optional rewarded ads stay.

The game requests non personalised ads by default, so the ads you see are chosen from the app and the general context rather than from a profile built about you across other apps and sites. AdMob and any network that serves an ad read the advertising identifier your device holds to cap how often you see the same ad and to detect fraudulent clicks. With each ad request they also receive your device's internet (IP) address, from which they can estimate a rough location such as your country or city, along with how you interact with the ad and information about the app's performance. The game tells each of those networks that you have not consented to personalised ads and that your data may not be sold or shared for cross-context advertising. We do not use the identifier to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, a consent form is shown before the first ad request, and your answer controls what AdMob and the networks it works with may store and use. Where the law gives you the choice, the Privacy screen in Options has an Ad privacy button to change it.

On iOS the game does not ask permission to track you, so the ad networks receive no advertising identifier from an iPhone or iPad and ads are served without one.

Your phone's own privacy settings let you reset your advertising identifier or turn off ad personalisation at any time.

# Your contact details
The Socials page in Options lets you add an email address or the handles of your social media accounts. They are optional, nothing in the game depends on them, and they are never used to sign in. We keep what you add with your player account and use it to get in touch if you win a prize. If you win a show board and added an email address, we email it about the prize whether or not its box is ticked, because that email is about your prize and not about shows. We do not post as you or contact your followers.

Each email address and handle has a box of its own: Okay to email me about shows under the email address, and Okay to contact me here about shows under each handle. They start ticked. While one is ticked, we may also contact you there about Morgan Jay's shows and the game. Emails are sent for us by an email delivery service, and every email has a link to stop them. Untick a box and we stop contacting you on that channel; the game remembers that you unticked it. You can also reply to any message and ask us to stop. Unticking never affects a prize.

So that we can read them, we keep a private copy of the contact details in a Google Sheets spreadsheet. It is rebuilt from our online service every day, so anything you remove or delete leaves the copy within a day.

Tap Remove on the Socials page at any time and we delete what you added. Delete my data removes it as well.

# Links out
The buttons on the main menu open Morgan Jay's pages, such as his site and his social channels, in your browser or their app. Once you are there, that company's own privacy policy applies.

# Who we share it with
Your data is not sold, and it is not shared for advertising or for anyone else's own purposes. These are the only recipients:

- Unity Gaming Services, part of Unity Technologies, which runs your player account, the leaderboards, your name, your ticket balance and upgrades, your contact details, your notification token, and our checks
- An email delivery service, which sends our prize emails to winners and, while the box is ticked, our emails about shows, and may not use your address for anything else
- Google, through Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Apple, through its push notification service, which deliver the prize notification to your phone if you allowed notifications
- Google, through Google Sheets, which holds the private copy of the contact details for us, rebuilt every day
- Other players, who can see your leaderboard name and the scores you post on the global and show boards
- Apple, through DeviceCheck, Game Center and the App Store, and Google, through Play Integrity, Play Games and Google Play, as described above
- Google, through AdMob, and AppLovin, DT Exchange (Digital Turbine), InMobi, ironSource, Liftoff, Meta, Mintegral, Pangle and Unity Technologies where AdMob passes them an ad request to fill
- Morgan Jay's tour crew, who check a winner's YOU WON screen and show ticket at the venue

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

# How long we keep it
Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the game. Your player account, your name, your ticket balance, your upgrades and your purchase record are kept until you delete your data. Contact details you added are kept until you remove them or delete your data, and leave the spreadsheet copy within a day after that. A notification token is kept until you delete your data or Firebase reports that it no longer works. Your scores are kept on the global board, and a show board and its archive are kept for the life of the tour, until you delete your data. A run that fails our checks is kept until it has been reviewed. A device's record that its free entries were used is kept until the end of that month, and a bar from prizes for 12 months.

# Deleting your data
Options has a Delete my data button. It deletes your player account, your name, your contact details, your notification token, your scores on every board, your ticket balance, your upgrades and your purchase record from our online service, and everything the game saved on your device. Your contact details leave our spreadsheet copy within a day. Tickets you bought are deleted with the rest and cannot be restored. Remove Ads can be restored from the store with Restore purchases, because the store keeps that purchase. If you are offline when you use it, the data on your device is deleted at once and the rest is removed the next time the game is online with the same account. The screen tells you which of those has happened.

Two things stay. A device's record that its free entries were used this month stays until the month ends, without your player account attached, so deleting and starting again does not give a phone a second free entry. And a bar from prizes on a device stays for its 12 months. Your Google Play Games or Apple Game Center account is not ours, and deleting your data leaves it untouched.

If you no longer have the game, you can ask for deletion through the page at https://apps.counterspell.dev/goofy-getaway/delete-your-data, or by writing to the address below with your leaderboard name.

# Your rights
Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how it is used, and to complain to your data protection authority. Delete my data is the fastest way to delete. For anything else, write to info@counterspell.dev and we will answer within 30 days.

# Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are requested at a general audience rating and the game is not flagged as child directed. If you believe a child has given us data, write to info@counterspell.dev and we will remove it.

# Changes to this policy
If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the version and effective date at the top change with it, and the published copy is updated at the same time. The copy inside the game moves with the next update; the current version is always the one at https://apps.counterspell.dev/goofy-getaway/privacy-policy.

# Sponsor
Show boards are run by Counterspell Games LLC, 10920 Rose Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90034, USA, the maker of Goofy Getaway (the Sponsor). Apple and Google are not sponsors of any show board or prize and are not involved in running them. Morgan Jay provides the prize experience described below.

# What a show board is
On a date when Morgan Jay performs and a show board is listed in the game, players at that venue can enter runs of Goofy Getaway on a board for that date. Early and late shows on the same date share one board. The highest entry when the board closes wins the prize for that date.

# Who can enter
You must be at least 13 years old, have a ticket for a show on that date at that venue, and live in a place where this contest is not prohibited. If you are under the age of majority where you live, you need a parent or guardian's permission to enter, and a parent or guardian must accompany you to collect the prize. Employees of the Sponsor and of Morgan Jay's touring company, and their immediate families, cannot win. Show boards run only in these countries: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Iceland, India, Indonesia, Italy, Luxembourg, Malaysia, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Singapore, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Thailand, Turkey, the United Kingdom and the United States. Void where prohibited.

# How to enter
At the venue, check in by typing the code printed on the card at the door into the game. Check in only works on the show date.

Play runs as normal. When a run ends while you are checked in and the board is open, the game offers to enter that run. Leaving the game over screen ends the offer for that run.

Each player can enter the board in these ways:

- One free entry. No purchase is needed to enter or to win, and the free entry has the same chance of winning as any other entry.
- One more entry for watching a rewarded ad.
- Further entries for tickets, at the price shown in the game. Tickets are earned by playing and can also be bought.

Only your best entered run counts on the board. A run can be entered only within 10 minutes of the game reporting it, and only if it reached our service while the run was fresh.

The free entry and the ad entry go to one player account per phone in each calendar month (UTC). A second account on the same phone in the same month can enter with tickets only.

# When boards open and close
Each board opens and closes at the times shown on it in the game, in the venue's local time. Entries that reach our service after the board closes are not counted.

# Winning
When the board closes, the entered run with the highest score is the provisional winner. If two entries have the same score, the one entered first wins. Before a winner is named, the top runs are checked automatically against the game's own records, including the device check described in the Privacy Policy, and any run those checks flag is reviewed by a person. A run that cannot be confirmed, or that breaks these rules or the Terms of Service, is disqualified and the next entry is checked.

The game tells the provisional winner with a YOU WON button on the main menu, a notification if they allowed notifications, and an email if they added an email address on the Socials page. To claim the prize the provisional winner must show the YOU WON screen in the game, and a ticket for a show on that date at that venue, to Morgan Jay's tour crew at the venue on the night. In Canada the winner must first correctly answer a mathematical skill testing question without help. A winner who cannot be confirmed or does not claim the prize on the night forfeits it.

# The prize
The prize for each show board is a meet and greet with Morgan Jay for the winner, at the venue after the show that night, and, at the Sponsor's discretion, Morgan Jay merchandise.

The prize is not money, has no cash alternative, cannot be transferred or sold, and is only available on the night at the venue. The Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if the stated prize cannot be provided.

# Your chances
Your chance of winning depends on your skill and on how many players enter and how well they play.

# If a show is cancelled or moved
If a show is cancelled, its board is cancelled and no prize is awarded for it. If a show moves to another date, its board moves with it. Runs entered on a cancelled board do not carry over, and tickets spent on entries to it are returned to your balance.

Using more than one account to get extra free entries, modifying or tampering with the game, automating play, entering runs you did not play yourself, or any other attempt to get an unfair result disqualifies the entry and can bar the account and the device from prizes for 12 months. The Sponsor's decisions on the results are final.
